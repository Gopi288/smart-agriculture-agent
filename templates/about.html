🧠 Deep Learning Vision Model

The image classifier utilizes MobileNetV2, an efficient convolutional neural network architecture developed by Google Research (Sandler et al., 2018).

Key Architectural Elements:
  • Inverted Residuals: Low-dimensional inputs expand to higher dimensions, apply depthwise convolutions, and project back to low dimensions.
  • Linear Bottlenecks: Prevents non-linearities (like ReLU) from destroying manifold information in low-dimensional feature representations.
  • Transfer Learning: Leverages ImageNet pre-trained representations fine-tuned specifically on agricultural foliar features.
  • CPU Efficiency: Only ~3.4M parameters; produces inference predictions in <50ms on standard student laptops.
💧 Agronomic Irrigation Model

The irrigation decision engine adapts scientific principles from FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper 56 and the USDA Natural Resources Conservation Service (NRCS):

Decision Factors:
  • Management Allowed Depletion (MAD): Crop-specific thresholds (Tomato: 35%, Potato: 40%, Corn: 30%).
  • Phenological Stage: Reproductive stages (Flowering/Tuber bulking) receive strict sensitivity adjustments.
  • Soil Texture Capacity: Sandy soils drain quickly (+6% trigger); clayey soils retain water (-5% trigger).
  • Precipitation Override: Rainfall ≥ 12 mm defers irrigation to prevent root hypoxia.
⚠️ Responsible AI, Limitations & Ethical Guardrails
Technical & Data Limitations:
  • Domain Shift: Model trained on curated laboratory leaves may exhibit lower confidence on field photos with soil backgrounds, shadows, or co-occurring pest bites.
  • Subterranean Pathogens: Visual classification identifies foliar symptoms only; root rots or vascular wilts cannot be confirmed from leaf imagery.
  • Scope Boundary: Supports 8 verified classes across 3 crops. Unsupported crops are rejected or flagged as out-of-distribution.
Responsible AI Measures:
  • Confidence Gating: Predictions below 70% confidence withhold treatment advice and advise physical extension inspection.
  • No Chemical Prescriptions: Does not prescribe chemical dosages or unregulated pesticides; provides cultural IPM measures only.
  • No Silent Zeroes: Missing soil sensor readings are explicitly reported as "Data Required", never fabricated as zero.
